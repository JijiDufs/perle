Privacy Policy

pearl-inc.app · Last updated: October 19, 2026

This page explains what information is collected when you use the pearl-inc.app website, published by Mathys Hosin, sole trader (see the legal notice).

The prototype

The playable prototype needs no account and sends us nothing. Your browser only keeps, on your device, your language, your trial oysters and your cookie choice.

Audience measurement

To know how many people visit the site, which pages they read and where they come from, we use Vercel Web Analytics. It sets no cookie and does not keep your IP address: each visit is counted anonymously, and we only see aggregate figures (page views, country, device and browser type, referring site).

Advertising cookies (X)

To see which ads on X (Twitter) bring visitors and sign-ups, we would like to use the X conversion pixel. It sets cookies and sends X the page address, your IP address, your browser type and, if you sign up, the fact that you signed up (never your email address). X processes them under its own privacy policy, including in the United States.

It only loads if you click "Accept" on the banner. If you decline, nothing is set. Your choice is kept for 6 months on your device; you can change it at any time with the Cookies link at the bottom of every page.

Launch sign-up

If you sign up to hear about the launch, we receive your email address, the phone you will use (optional) and where your visit came from (social network, campaign). We use them only to tell you when Pearl Inc. comes out, give you the sign-up skin and see which ad worked. We never sell or share them.

The form is run by Tally (Belgium), which stores the data in the European Union. It is deleted at the latest 6 months after the game's release. You sign up with your consent (under 15: with a parent's consent) and can withdraw it at any time.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel, which briefly keeps technical logs (IP address, browser) for security. The font comes from Google Fonts and a drawing library from cdnjs (Cloudflare): these services see your IP address.

Your rights

To access, correct or delete your data, or to unsubscribe, email us at laplateformemjh@gmail.com: we answer within one month. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr), or to the authority of your country.

Changes

This page will be updated when the Pearl Inc. app comes out. The date at the top shows the latest version.
