## 4. Notes pour la revue d'Apple (en anglais)

```
Pearl Inc. is a casual game: swipe up to open an oyster and reveal a pearl. The app is in English and French (it follows the device language).

Sign-in: no credentials needed. On the first screen, tap "Play without an account". Sign in with Apple and email codes are also available.

First minutes: the first 4 oysters are a short scripted tutorial (they are free). The tabs, counters and missions appear after it.

iPhone Duo: on iPhone Duo (iOS 27.1), the hinge angle opens the oyster; fold the phone shut to bring the next oyster. On any other iPhone, the same game is played by swiping up.

Odds: the probability of every pearl is shown before any purchase, in the Shop ("Every oyster is a draw: see the odds") and in Settings > "Pearl odds". A golden pearl or better is guaranteed within 26 oysters. Shells and purchases never change the odds.

Ads: rewarded ads only (Google AdMob, non-personalised, no tracking, no ATT prompt), always optional: "Watch an ad" gives 3 oysters, up to 3 times a day. Google's consent form appears where required (EU); "Ad privacy" in Settings lets the player change it.

In-app purchases: oysters and gold dust (consumables), two cosmetic shells (non-consumables, restorable with "Restore purchases" in the Shop and in Settings).

Notifications: never requested at launch; only offered when the oyster stock is empty, or in Settings.

Account deletion: Settings (gear icon on the fishing screen) > "Delete my account".
```

## 5. Ce que la fiche ne doit pas dire (pas dans la 1.0)

- « iPhone » ou « Duo » dans le **nom**, le **sous-titre** ou les **mots-clés** (règles d'Apple) ; la description peut parler de l'iPhone Duo.
- Classement Game Center, sauvegarde iCloud (« ta collection te suit »), vibrations « riches » (Core Haptics).
- Écrins, bijoux, atelier, Maison, niveaux : c'est la webapp, pas l'app.
- Le skin **Première marée** (inscrits) : pas encore distribuable (voir `docs/duo-gadget.md` §6).

À chaque fonction ajoutée, compléter la description et le texte promotionnel ici (dans les deux langues) avant de les mettre dans App Store Connect.
