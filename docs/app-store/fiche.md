## 4. Notes pour la revue d'Apple (en anglais)

```
Pearl Inc. is a casual game: swipe up to open an oyster and reveal a pearl. The app is in English and French (it follows the device language).

Sign-in: no credentials needed. On the first screen, tap "Play without an account". Sign in with Apple and email codes are also available.

First minutes: the first 4 oysters are a short scripted tutorial (they are free). The tabs, counters and missions appear after it.

iPhone Duo: on iPhone Duo (iOS 27.1), the hinge angle opens the oyster (and the jewel case); fold the phone shut to bring the next one. On any other iPhone, the same game is played by swiping up.

What's new in 1.1: jewel cases (the "Jewel cases" tab appears with the first case: one every 12 oysters opened and one at every new rank, or 200 gold dust at the counter, or as an in-app purchase). Swipe up to open the case; hold still where the jewel sparkles for a perfect glint. Also: levels and ranks, 41 achievements, a richer collection (Pearls / Jewels / Finds), and the game is saved to the player's account (Supabase, EU).

Odds: the probability of every pearl and every jewel is shown before any purchase: in the Shop (oysters: "Every oyster is a draw: see the odds"; jewel cases: the ⓘ "see the odds" line above the jewel case purchases), on the Jewel cases tab ("Odds" button) and in Settings. A golden pearl or better is guaranteed within 26 oysters, a ★ jewel or better within 21 jewel cases. Bought jewel cases are drawn exactly like earned ones. Shells and purchases never change the odds.

Thief fish: now and then (shown in the odds: 3% of pearls, any rarity), a fish snatches the pearl that was just revealed. Tapping the fish while it swims away gets the pearl back (+2 gold dust); otherwise the pearl is lost. It swims twice as slowly with VoiceOver or Reduce Motion. It is random: it may take 30 to 40 oysters to see it.

Ads: rewarded ads only (Google AdMob, non-personalised, no tracking, no ATT prompt), always optional: "Watch an ad" gives 3 oysters, up to 3 times a day. No consent or cookie prompt is shown: Google's consent message is unpublished, and the app does not track users.

In-app purchases: oysters, jewel cases and gold dust (consumables), two cosmetic shells (non-consumables, restorable with "Restore purchases" in the Shop and in Settings).

Notifications: never requested at launch; only offered when the oyster stock is empty, or in Settings.

Account deletion: Settings (gear icon on the fishing screen) > "Delete my account".
```

## 5. Ce que la fiche ne doit pas dire (pas dans la 1.1)

- « iPhone » ou « Duo » dans le **nom**, le **sous-titre** ou les **mots-clés** (règles d'Apple) ; la description peut parler de l'iPhone Duo.
- Classement Game Center, sauvegarde **iCloud** (la sauvegarde passe par le compte Pearl Inc., pas par iCloud), vibrations « riches » (Core Haptics).
- Expertise à la loupe, Maison de Rose, atelier, carte des lagons : c'est la webapp, pas l'app (lots suivants, `docs/ios-feuille-de-route.md`).
- Le skin **Première marée** : réservé aux inscrits d'avant la sortie, il ne s'achète pas ; ne pas l'annoncer comme un contenu à obtenir.
- « Nouveau » dans les visuels : ils restent en ligne plusieurs versions ; la nouveauté se dit dans le texte promotionnel et les nouveautés de version.

À chaque fonction ajoutée, compléter la description, les nouveautés et le texte promotionnel ici (dans les deux langues) avant de les mettre dans App Store Connect.
